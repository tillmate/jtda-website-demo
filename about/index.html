About JTDA

Enabling Jamaica through technology since 1975.

We champion the effective use of IT for the benefit of all — linking ICT experts, professionals, academia, enterprise and enthusiasts for new thinking, education and career growth.

Our vision

To enable people and businesses by providing access, influence, and empowerment through technology.

01

Access

Open doors to knowledge, tools and the people shaping Jamaica’s ICT industry.

  • Events & Member’s Mingles
  • Skills Lab & JCSI cyber training
  • Member perks & partner discounts
02

Influence

One voice for technology — to government, industry and the public.

  • Policy advocacy since 1995
  • CIO Forum for technology leaders
  • The BizTech Conference stage
03

Empowerment

Careers, credentials and connections that help people and businesses grow.

  • Mentorship & executive boards
  • Member Plus with BCS & CPD
  • Youth Mingle for under-30s

Our story

Five decades, one mission.

Incorporated in 1975 as the Jamaica Computer Society. Since our 2021 merger with JITSA, we are the Jamaica Technology & Digital Alliance.

Read the rename story
1973

Programmers unite

Local programmers start the Jamaica Computer Society.

1975

Incorporated

The JCS is legally incorporated — our founding year.

1995

Advocacy

Calls on government to invest in technology.

1999

Musgrave Medal

A national honour for merit in technology.

2021

Joined forces
