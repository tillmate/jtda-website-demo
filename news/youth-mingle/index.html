JTDA Youth Mingle: an evening for Jamaica’s next tech leaders

An evening for students, young professionals and the under-30 tech community to connect and plug into the wider JTDA network.

Find your community

Youth Mingle brings together students, young professionals and people under 30 for an evening of connection. Danielle Mullings, Deputy President and Youth Council Lead, hosts the programme.

Start a conversation

The evening format runs from 5:30 to 9:00 PM and is free for students. The last recorded event in this brief was held on 25 September 2026. Watch the events page for confirmed details of the next one.
