The JTDA CIO Forum: connect, share, lead

A free, quarterly virtual roundtable where CIOs and director-level technology leaders compare notes.

A free, quarterly virtual roundtable where CIOs and director-level technology leaders compare notes.

Connect. Share. Lead.

The CIO Forum is a quarterly virtual peer roundtable for CIOs, VPs and director-level technology leaders, hosted by JTDA’s Innovation & Education unit. Topics are chosen in advance to make space for focused, practical discussion.

A space for technology leaders

The forum is free and members only, with sessions scheduled from 7:45 to 9:00 AM. Contact the JTDA team to ask about the next roundtable.
