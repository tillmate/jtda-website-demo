A little clarity

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about joining, renewing and growing with JTDA.

Talk to the team
Who can join JTDA?

Students, professionals, MSMEs and corporate organisations all have a place at JTDA. Choose a plan based on where you are in your journey.

How much does membership cost?

Fees for Student, Professional, MSME and Corporate membership are confirmed on application. Published Member Plus annual fees are J$16,344 for IT Associate, J$26,050 for IT Professional and J$31,828 for IT Fellow.

What is Member Plus?

Member Plus pairs JTDA membership with BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT, with skills frameworks, career tools, learning and professional development.

Can an organisation add its team?

The redesigned MSME and Corporate experience includes organisation accounts and team-seat management. Seat allowances and pricing need to be confirmed by JTDA.

Can I join and pay in this preview?

You can review the application experience. Live membership approval, payment and card issuance will be connected before launch.
